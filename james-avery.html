James Avery / Charm Pick-Place & Powder Dispense

JA-32E-001
32E CHARM SYSTEM · DDR RENDER

PROJECT DESCRIPTION

Autonomous charm finishing line: Texas Feeder loads blank pallets, Digitool-01 dispenses a uniform powder curtain across each tray, and Digitool-02 picks singulated charms from a vibe table and places them onto the powder-coated pallets via gantry, vacuum, and rotary tweezer grippers.

Vision-driven throughout — Helios2+ ToF for charm detection and CAD-mesh fitment, Basler Ace cameras for pick & place validation. Targets ~3,600 charms per 12-hour shift with operator-loaded bins on a 3m belt conveyor.

THROUGHPUT~300 charms/hr
FOOTPRINT4.1m × 3.3m
PLATFORM2× Digitool + Texas
SHIFT VOLUME~3,600 charms
